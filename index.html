FAQ

Which links work?

Standard watch URLs, youtu.be short links, Shorts and embed links. Private, age-restricted or deleted videos cannot be fetched.

Why is 1080p sometimes slower?

YouTube stores high resolutions as separate video and audio streams, so the tool merges them into one MP4 — it takes a few extra seconds.

Where do files go?

Straight into your browser's normal download folder, with the video's title as the file name.

Is this okay to use?

Download videos you own, that are Creative Commons, or where you have the creator's permission. Respect copyright.
